inside_desc: |
  Our inside is composed of three primary spaces: our Dining Room and two private rooms: The Penthouse and The Executive Room.

  The Dining Room can accommodate anywhere from 50 to 100 guests.

  The Penthouse, originally built as our grandfather's residence, sits above our dining room and is approximately 1,500 square feet. It features a full-service bar, sweeping views of Lake Union and the Cascade Mountains, and a baby grand piano. It can accommodate up to 90 guests.

  The Executive Room is 400 square feet of warmth and comfort. Located on our main level, it features a fireplace and north- and east-facing views of Lake Union and the Cascade Mountains through 10-foot-high windows. It can accommodate up to 24 guests seated.

  Depending on the size of your event, these spaces can be reserved individually, combined in different configurations, or enjoyed alongside other private events—or even our regular dinner service.
outside_desc: |
  The Canlis parking lot stretches across two terraced tiers—nearly 10,000 square feet carved into the hillside. The upper level enjoys expansive views of Lake Union, while the lower level feels tucked away and intimate.

  During the pandemic, we transformed our parking lot from simply a place to leave cars into a canvas for creativity, hospitality, and community. It became an experimental stage where we reimagined what a restaurant could be when indoor dining was no longer possible.

  Over the years we've built a drive-in movie theater, a seated crab shack, an outdoor barbecue restaurant, hosted intimate concerts, and served pizzas and burgers to thousands—all across this unlikely stretch of asphalt.
occasion_brief: When it's time to go even bigger than big, we're here for it.
mid_brief: |
  For seventy five years, we have had the great honor of hosting our guest’s most treasured occasions. Reflecting on those events, we knew we could serve you in an even bigger way, and are happy to now offer the opportunity for full restaurant buyouts. 

  We’ve thrown some great parties ourselves over the years, and will give your event that same care and attention. A buyout of the restaurant means the sky is the limit. While we may not let you paint the restaurant pink like we did, we would love to help you imagine something that will wow your guests just as much. 

  <i>Full buyouts require a 6-month minimum lead time and are approved on a case-by-case basis.</i>
